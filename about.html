Overview

Bridging Biotechnology and Agriculture

BHARTI GREEN TECH is an Indian agricultural biotechnology company dedicated to building research-backed, high-efficacy crop feed and biological protection solutions. Based out of our registered office in Navi Mumbai and manufacturing units in Satara, Maharashtra, we formulate biological solutions that enrich the soil, stimulate seeds, and fortify foliage against fungal or pest stresses.

We operate with a farmer-first philosophy. Rather than relying on generic chemicals that damage soil carbon over time, our laboratory processes isolate specific microbial organisms, including Nitrogen-fixers, Phosphate solubilizers, and entomopathogenic fungi. These are refined into water-soluble dextrose carrier powders, stable liquid consortia, and slow-release granules for diverse crop requirements.

Our History

Milestones & Growth Timeline

How BHARTI GREEN TECH expanded its research and product line to support farming communities.

2020

Company Foundation

Establishment of BHARTI GREEN TECH in Navi Mumbai, focusing on isolating beneficial soil-borne bacterial strains (Azotobacter, PSB).

2022

Manufacturing Expansion

Commissioning of our dedicated formulation plant in Satara, Maharashtra, setting up high-volume liquid fermentation vats.

2024

Dextrose-Solubles Launch

Release of our water-soluble dextrose base powder line (Urva Fungo, Urva Wilto, Urva Nutri, Urva K-Plus), receiving excellent feedback from pomegranate growers.

2026

Supporting 50,000+ Farmers

Supplying 26 primary biological products plus granular lines to over 50,000 farmers nationwide via distributor networks.
